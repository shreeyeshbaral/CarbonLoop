## 1. Welcome to CarbonLoop

CarbonLoop is an operating system engineered to eliminate institutional e-waste and redundant capital expenditure across **ITER SOA University, Bhubaneswar**.

### Key Architectural Concepts:
- **Circular Economy**: Retiring equipment from one faculty (e.g. *ITER CSE Block 1 / C-block*) is automatically routed to fulfill procurement shortages in neighboring faculties (e.g. *Bansuri Guru Design Studio*).
- **Zero Saturated Blues/Purples**: Engineered in a warm, premium coffee-white (`#F7F4EC`), crisp surface (`#FFFDF8`), and forest sustainability (`#176B3A`) design system.
- **Multi-Device Support**: Optimized with responsive layouts for mobile phones, tablets, and laptops.

## 4. Module 2: Asset Marketplace & Live Editing (`/assets`)

Search, claim, edit, and declare institutional hardware and laboratory assets.

### How to Filter & Search Assets:
1. Use the **Search Bar** to type asset names (e.g. *"Dell Latitude"*), asset tags (*"ASSET-CSE-104"*), manufacturers (*"Herman Miller"*), or model numbers.
2. Filter simultaneously across **Category** (*Laptops, Monitors, Chairs, Desks, Projectors, Networking, Lab Equipment*), **Department** (*CSE, MECH, ECE, CIVIL, DESIGN, LIBRARY, ADMIN, RESEARCH*), **Condition** (*EXCELLENT, GOOD, FAIR, POOR*), and **Action**.

### How to Declare a New Surplus Asset / E-Waste:
1. Click the **"+ Declare Surplus Asset / E-Waste"** button in the header.
2. Fill out the intake form:
   - Equipment Name (e.g. *"HP LaserJet Enterprise M608dn"*)
   - Category & Manufacturer
   - Hardware Condition (`EXCELLENT`, `GOOD`, `FAIR`, `POOR`)
   - Assigned ITER Faculty Block & Room Number
   - Estimated Retained Value (₹) & Repair Cost (₹)
3. *(Optional)* Click **"Analyze with AI"** to auto-classify raw text descriptions.
4. Click **"Confirm Surplus Intake"** $\longrightarrow$ The asset immediately appears in the live catalog and on the campus map!

### How to Edit Any Existing Asset Live:
1. Locate any asset card and click the **✏️ Pencil Icon** in the top-right corner of the card.
2. Modify equipment name, condition, assigned department building, valuation in ₹, or circular pathway.
3. Click **"Save Changes"** to update the database in real time.
4. *(Optional)* Click **"Delete E-Waste"** to permanently decommission defective hardware.

### How to Claim an Asset:
- Click **"Claim Asset →"** on any card to submit an internal requisition.

5. **Inspect Chain of Custody**: View the immutable chronological activity log at the bottom of the dossier.

## 7. Module 5: Matching Engine & Shortage Requisitions (`/intelligence`)

Autonomous algorithmic pairing connecting departmental surplus with active campus procurement needs.

### How to Declare a Department Equipment Shortage:
1. Click **"+ Declare Equipment Shortage"** in the header.
2. Select your requesting department (e.g. *ITER Bansuri Guru Design Studio*).
3. Select required category (e.g. *Monitors*), quantity needed (e.g. *4 units*), and urgency level (`CRITICAL`, `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`).
4. Enter operational justification (e.g. *"Design fellowship lab workstations expanding for incoming batch"*).
5. Click **"Post Shortage Requisition"** $\longrightarrow$ The Matching Engine instantly computes live pairings!

### How the Deterministic Matching Formula Works:
$$\text{MatchScore} = \text{Compatibility (40\%)} + \text{Condition (25\%)} + \text{Proximity (20\%)} + \text{Urgency (15\%) }$$

- **Compatibility (40 pts)**: Category alignment.
- **Condition (25 pts)**: Hardware condition meets/exceeds requester's minimum standard.
- **Proximity (20 pts)**: Haversine distance between ITER campus blocks.
- **Urgency (15 pts)**: Priority scaling for emergency requisitions.

### How to Filter & Dispatch Matches:
1. Drag the **"Minimum Match Score"** slider (e.g. 70%) to filter higher-confidence pairings.
2. Filter by **Source Department** or **Target Department**.
3. Inspect the detailed 4-bar score breakdown on each opportunity card.
4. Click **"Initiate Custody Transfer"** $\longrightarrow$ Dispatches the transfer to the **Route Optimizer**!

   - **Optimized Loop Distance**: `3.42 km` total transit distance.
   - **Van Turnaround Time**: `18 mins` turnaround.
   - **Avoided Van Fuel Emissions**: `1.84 kg CO₂` combustion emissions prevented.
3. **Inspect the Interactive Routing Map**:
   - View the sequence order: **ITER Admin Block (Depot) $\to$ C-block (Data Science) $\to$ D-block (ECE) $\to$ Bansuri Guru Wing $\to$ Central Library $\to$ Admin Block**.
